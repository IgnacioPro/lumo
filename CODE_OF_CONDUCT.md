# Code of Conduct

Lumo is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Assuming good intent and collaborating in good faith
- Prioritizing the health of the community and project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful language
- Personal attacks, trolling, or intimidation
- Publishing private information without consent
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion in bad faith

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

To report violations, follow the security/support contact details in `SECURITY.md` and `SUPPORT.md`.

All reports will be reviewed and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.
